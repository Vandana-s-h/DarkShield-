    Don't just detect the threat.
    Explain the manipulation.

    Traditional warnings tell users a link is suspicious. DarkShield combines technical threat signals, social-engineering signals, rule-based correlation, explainable risk assessment, and defensive guidance — so people understand why something is dangerous, not just that it is.
